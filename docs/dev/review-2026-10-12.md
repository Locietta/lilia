MaiBot was read at `0f94cfa8` (2026-10-12), 141 commits after the `f9ec367`

## Part 1: behaviour gaps

1. **The writer gets the planner's analysis, not what it found.** ✔
   - MaiBot now requires a non-empty `reply_reference` and calls the replyer
     with `reply_reason=""` (`src/maisaka/builtin_tool/reply.py:130-137,
353-359, 449`, commit `908dfa6e`, 2026-10-09). The analysis no longer
     reaches the replyer.
   - lilia started forwarding the analysis on 2026-10-11 (`75e106c`) from the
     stale notes. It is decision talk ("她应该…因为…").
   - The recalled summary only reaches the planner tail (`chat/turn.cpp:232`).
   - `look_up_person` results reach the writer only for the target, and then
     as the newest six facts (`chat/reply.cpp:117`).
   - `intent` stays the default handoff: it was chosen over `reference` after
     replay comparisons (`f08026e`).
2. **One tool per round, 8 rounds.** ✔
   - lilia refuses a second call in a round (`chat/turn.cpp:159`).
   - MaiBot runs every call of a response in order and stops after `wait`
     or a stopping tool (`src/maisaka/reasoning_engine.py:2047-2112`). Its
     cap is 16 rounds (`src/maisaka/runtime.py:75`, `074a8f41`; it was 10).
   - In lilia each lookup costs a planner round, and a reply plus a sticker
     takes three.
3. **Mid-term recall works from the wrong summaries.**
   - MaiBot summarises exactly what is trimmed from the planner window
     (`prompts/zh-CN/mid_term_memory_summary.prompt`: "完整摘要，保留话题脉络、
     人物立场、已达成结论、待办和重要细节，适合之后恢复上下文"). The newest 10
     stay in the planner's history as "[聊天回想]" blocks, and the best cue
     match (0.8, last 12 messages) is recalled as a reference
     (`src/maisaka/memory/mid_term.py`).
   - lilia's 36-message rolling summaries are sparse long-term notes, allowed
     to be empty (`prompts/summary.md`). They are cut independently of the
     window, and recall compares the last 6 lines against all of them.
4. **Private chats run the group prompts.**
   - `prompts/planner.md:1` says "你在旁观一个 QQ 群聊", and rules like
     "拿不准…就当作不是" apply in DMs. `writer.md` says "你正在 QQ 群里聊天".
   - MaiBot swaps in its private chat prompt
     (`src/maisaka/chat_loop_service.py:808-823`), and a new private message
     ends a wait (`src/maisaka/turn_trigger/scheduler.py:53-56`). lilia's wait
     is a fixed sleep.
5. **No anti-loop guard.** MaiBot replaces an analysis that is more than 90%
   similar to the previous one with a reflection prompt
   (`reasoning_engine.py:438-447`). lilia only has the prompt line "只写新的判断".
6. **The planner knows people only on request.**
   - MaiBot (with A_Memorix) injects sectioned profiles for up to 3 people per
     round.
   - lilia shows the newest 6 facts by date, not by relevance ✔, without
     dates. Near-duplicate updates about others keep the stale fact.
7. **Smaller gaps:**
   - lilia cannot @ anyone.
   - Quoting is opt-in in lilia; MaiBot quotes by default for older targets.
   - Facts from private chats are not scoped away from groups.

**Keep (lilia has these and MaiBot does not):**

- addressee arrows and quote structure, the fitted addressee prior, hindsight
  labels
- sticker retrieval with learned usage
- verbatim-quote fact checks (MaiBot removed its own) and self facts
- history search on by default, web search
- prompt caching layout, replay probes

## Part 2: ad hoc parts

How much: about 20–25% of `chat/` code, about half of the `planner.md` and
`writer.md` rules, and about 5% of learn/memory/sticker/feedback code (a
quarter of its behaviour knobs).

**Revisit:**

1. **Addressee is solved five times:**
   - planner prose about 你/你们
   - tail facts repeating `→她` (`chat/turn.cpp:264-303`)
   - `interaction_summary` dyads (`chat/addressee.cpp:96-156`)
   - a different dyad definition and keyword lists in `line_features`
   - two writer patches (`writer.md:1`, `chat/context.cpp:280`)

   Keep the structural edges. Make the fitted prior the one derived signal
   (addressee recognition: Ouchi & Tsuboi 2016, Le et al. 2019, MPC-BERT).

2. **Whether to speak is prose rate control**: "说过一两次了就先让别人聊",
   "隔一阵冒个泡", "wait 一般 5 到 20 秒", lists of reaction words. Use a fitted
   P(reply) under a budget (MaiBot's dynamic mode), or a contextual bandit on
   the hindsight reward.
3. **Writer rules quote what they forbid**: "确实""欢迎入坑", a verbatim
   "炫彩还有保质期？", 草/www, and the same sticker-rule priming. The
   friendliness rule appears three times. A/B rules in replay before adding
   them.
4. **Output scrubbing chain**: `strip_speaker_label`, `strip_leading_mention`,
   `strip_asides`, `strip_transcript`, character caps, emoji exemption. The
   root cause is the `name: text` writer format. Fix the input, then use one
   validator that retries and counts its repairs.
5. **Four stacked delays**: debounce, presence read moment, busy schedule,
   image wait. Replace them with one latency distribution fitted to real gaps.
6. **Planner dispatch**: an if-chain on tool names with ad hoc counters, and
   silent fixes for planner errors (a self target becomes the newest message,
   a missing wait becomes 30 s). `must_speak` is test logic in the production
   loop.
7. **Expression blocklists** (`learn/learner.cpp:124-138,215-222`) match
   substrings and are patched twice. `learner.md` contradicts itself on 梗, and
   styles are not grounded against the source line.
8. **Sticker pool freezes** ✔ (`sticker/sticker.cpp:542`): a newcomer replaces
   only a never-sent member. Counts never decay, and reward is a running sum.
   Use decayed counts and a Beta/Bernoulli reward.
9. **Feedback reward table** (`feedback/feedback.cpp:35-44`) is hand-set. The
   `--eval` path scores judge labels with settings that differ from production.

**Fine, don't churn:**

- the delivery and typos ports, the planner history and caching
- edges and quotes, fact grounding, jargon and sticker usage cycles
- the retriever core and the evaluated reranking, the logistic prior fitting
- privacy scrubbing

**Stale docs:**

- `maibot-notes.md`: reply reference fallback, 10 rounds, behaviour learning
- `config.h` threshold comments still name Qwen3-Embedding-0.6B
- `select.md` says 0–3 picks while the code allows 5; `jargon.md` length limits
  differ from the code

## Fixes 1–4 (`8f93218`, not deployed)

**Verified (2026-10-12):**

- Local and cachy test lanes pass, sanitizer lane clean.
- Replays on cachy with the live models:
  - addressee probes ×2: intrusions 0/24, misses 0/16, to_her 18/18 (same
    as before)
  - `ai-talk` and `long-chat`: short replies; "你到时候记得提醒我" now
    answered
  - `long-chat` with `context_messages` 8: three window summaries, each a
    coherent who-said-what with open points marked as open
- The planner never combined calls in these replays (Haiku ends with a
  separate round).
- The replays showed the planner taking its per-round notes for part of her
  last line. Fixed in the same commit: a separator before the notes, and
  `planner.md` describes them.

1. **Writer handoff** (`chat/context.cpp`, `chat/reply.cpp`, `chat/turn.cpp`).
   - The analysis no longer reaches the writer. `intent` stays.
   - A `Findings` struct carries what the turn found to the writer as facts:
     the recalled summary, `search_history` and `search_web` results, the
     target's facts, and everyone `look_up_person` looked up.
2. **Several calls per round** (`chat/turn.cpp`).
   - Calls run in order, one receipt each, and every call is logged in
     `planner_turns`.
   - `wait` skips the rest of the round's calls and `stay_silent` ends the
     turn; the skipped calls are answered "没有执行…".
   - 16 rounds. The limits on replies, waits and lookups per turn stay.
   - `planner.md` says how to combine calls.
3. **Window summaries** (`memory/summaries.cpp`, `store/`, schema 17).
   - `chat_summaries.kind` is `rolling` or `window`.
   - Every time `log_window()` sets an anchor, `summarize_window` runs over
     the uncovered lines up to it (at most 2×`context_messages`) with
     `prompts/window_summary.md`.
   - The newest 10 lead the planner's log, in the cached part. Recall
     searches the older window summaries only; rolling ones stay for search.
   - Still to do: re-measure `recall_score` on window cues
     (`tests/replay/recall.jsonl`).
4. **Private chats.**
   - `{situation}` in `planner.md` / `writer.md` is filled from
     `planner_group.md` / `planner_private.md` and `writer_group.md` /
     `writer_private.md`.
   - A new private message ends a planner wait (`ChatState::arrival`).
